Research Engineer · Foundation models

I build foundation models, and the infrastructure that trains them.

At BharatGen I designed the task taxonomy and built the question‑answer data for Patram 1.5, the team's document foundation model, which now has a 41.3-billion-token training corpus and a 7B model training on 128 GPUs. I first-authored DocLayout‑VL, a layout foundation model accepted to ECCV 2026. And I wrote the infrastructure under both: the storage layer, the data-pipeline engine, the training framework, and the cluster tooling the team runs on.
