Research Engineer · BharatGen

Srihari Bandarupalli

I build foundation vision‑language models and the large‑scale systems behind them.

I'm a Research Engineer at BharatGen. Most recently I trained Patram 1.5, our foundation model for document understanding, and built the data generation behind two of its new capabilities, OCR 3.0 and abstention. Before that I was first author of DocLayout‑VL, a layout model accepted at ECCV 2026.

I enjoy moving between model research and systems engineering. A training experiment can turn into a data problem, a memory bottleneck or a distributed‑systems problem, and working out how those pieces fit together is the part I like most. That is how I came to build much of the stack under our models: the storage layer, the data‑generation engine, the training framework and the cluster they run on.

Just released · Nov 2026

Patram 1.5

Built in India, best in the world.

BharatGen's 7‑billion‑parameter vision‑language model for document intelligence. Of the 21 vision‑language models we tested, none scores higher, and it matches the largest of them at 97 times smaller.

It brings two new capabilities: OCR 3.0, where you ask for exactly what you need from a page in the format you need it, and abstention, the ability to say "I don't know". The weights are open under Apache 2.0.
