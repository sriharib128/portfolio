About me

I'm a Research Engineer at BharatGen, working on foundation vision‑language models and the large‑scale systems behind them. Recently that has meant building and training Patram 1.5, our foundation model for document understanding, which took model training, data generation and the infrastructure to bring them together.

I care about both what a model can do and how efficiently we can build and run it at scale, so I move between the two. Before BharatGen I worked on enterprise vision‑language models at Sprinklr and on data‑efficient speech recognition at LTRC, IIIT Hyderabad.
